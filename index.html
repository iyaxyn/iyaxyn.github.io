Whiteout uploads

Personal tool of Stanislav Arnaut (iyaxyn@gmail.com). It uploads recordings of my own Whiteout Survival play sessions to my own YouTube channel as private videos and adds them to a private playlist.

Privacy policy

The app is used only by its owner. It accesses only the owner's YouTube account to upload private videos and manage playlists. No data of other people is collected, stored, sold or shared. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Terms of service

Private, non-commercial personal use by the owner only. No service is offered to third parties.
